How To Play

Click Play then click the game window to lock your mouse. Move with your movement keys, aim with the mouse, and fire with your shoot key.

  • 1-5 — Switch weapon slots (gun, melee)
  • Left Click — Fire / Swing
  • Right Click — Aim down sights
  • R — Reload
  • Shift — Sprint
  • Space — Jump
  • Ctrl — Crouch
  • Esc — Pause / unlock mouse

All keys are rebindable from the Settings screen. Bots patrol, hunt, and shoot back — take them out before they take you out. Highest kill count wins the round.
